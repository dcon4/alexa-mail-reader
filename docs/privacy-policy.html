Privacy Policy

Email Reader, an Alexa skill. Last updated November 14, 2026.

This policy explains what the Email Reader skill accesses, stores, and shares. Contact for privacy questions: dcon2000@gmail.com.

What the skill accesses

When you link your Google account, the skill requests only these permissions:

  • Read email — to list and read your Gmail messages aloud when you ask.
  • Send email — to send the single message you composed or replied to, and only after you hear it read back and say "send it." The skill never sends mail on its own.
  • Contacts (autocomplete pool) — to match a person's name you say out loud to their email address when you compose. The skill does not read your full contact list or store it.

What is stored and where

  • During a conversation, your current reading position, message list, and any unsent draft are held in the session with Alexa. Unsent drafts are discarded when the session ends.
  • Your reading position (message identifiers and subjects) is kept on the skill's server so "welcome back" works across sessions, and can be removed on request.
  • The skill's server keeps diagnostic logs of the conversation flow (commands given and responses spoken), which may include message subjects or sender names that were spoken aloud. Logs are kept only as long as needed to debug problems and are deleted on request.

What is shared

  • Message text is sent to Amazon's Alexa service to be spoken to you, as with any Alexa skill.
  • The skill talks only to Google's Gmail and Contacts APIs and to Amazon's Alexa platform. Nothing is sold, and no advertising or third-party analytics trackers are used.

Security

Your Google sign-in is handled by Google and Amazon's standard account linking. The skill receives a limited, revocable access token — your Google password is never seen or stored by the skill.

Revoking access

  • Unlink in the Alexa app: More → Skills & Games → Email Reader → Settings → Disable skill.
  • Remove the Google permission at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

The skill is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, this page will be updated with a new date at the top.
